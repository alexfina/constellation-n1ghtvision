# UI Concept

## Visual Direction

Constellation should feel simple, modern, playful, and elegant.

## Core Idea

The selection flow should feel like forming a constellation:

- each user choice becomes a star
- the final recommendation reveal feels like a guided pattern, not a gamble
- the animation should feel calm and intentional

## Principles

- Avoid casino-style roulette or slot-machine behavior
- Keep the interface minimal and readable
- Use subtle motion, not noisy effects
- Make the reveal feel rewarding without being random-looking
- Keep the design friendly for beginners and easy to build in small steps

## UI Building Blocks

- Clear filter controls
- A small starfield or constellation canvas
- A concise recommendation results area
- Gentle transitions and reveal effects
